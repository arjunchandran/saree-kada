# Demo product data

The local preview catalog is in `public/products.json`. When Firebase is configured, the site fetches the `products` collection from Firestore; see `FIREBASE_SETUP.md` for setup.

Products with `demo: true` are illustrative examples, not real items for sale. Their prices and garment details are invented. The shop filters use `collective-category` for garment type and `product-category` for occasion.

The demo garment descriptions are informed by general information about [kurtas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurta) and [clothing in India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clothing_in_India). Their illustrative photos are from Pexels: [blue kurti](https://www.pexels.com/photo/young-woman-wearing-a-blue-kurti-19556879/), [white shirt](https://www.pexels.com/photo/woman-wearing-white-shirt-1181690/), [orange skirt](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-woman-in-an-orange-skirt-and-top-27954765/), and [traditional outfit](https://www.pexels.com/photo/model-in-blue-salwar-kameez-dress-posing-on-steps-25184992/).
